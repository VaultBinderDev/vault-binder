The Vault Binder System

1. Create a Binder

Start My Collection

Through the Collect Cards at the top and the button above, you'll be met with an Add Card Section. Through that section you'll be able to add your pokemon cards, and display them in your binder.

Your binder can soon be imported and exported to another device in case you find it much easier to add images from your phones/tablets instead of computers.

The binders include multiple sections including tabs, pages, slots, and the cards themselves. Your whole binder consists of multiple tabs, and soon you'll be able to rename them. Tabs can help for more organization later on. Giving you more space to hold your cards.

2. Add Pages

Each page within the tabs can hold multiple cards/slots. Pages can be added until the max amount. You can either use the arrows to swipe between each page or the slider at the bottom to flip between the pages.

3. Fill Slots

The slots and cards are going to be different soon, allowing you to drag the cards around from slot to slot. Each added card from the top most section will be added into the first slots going in descending order.

4. Build Collections

As you add cards and create more tabs you can continue to build your collection outwards. Later on more support for card rating/rarities/value will be available for cards.
